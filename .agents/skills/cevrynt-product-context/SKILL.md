# Cevrynt Product Context

## Positioning

- Present Cevrynt as an early-stage AI underwriting and decision-intelligence platform for U.S. alternative lenders, initially focused on merchant cash advance and SMB finance.
- Use the preferred line: **From borrower documents to decision-ready underwriting.**
- Explain that Cevrynt structures borrower documents and business signals into analysis for human underwriters.
- Describe the workflow consistently: **Intake → Documents → Financials → Verification → Fraud → Policy → Report → Human Decision**.
- State that Cevrynt is AI-assisted infrastructure, not a lender. Lenders retain final approval authority.

## Capabilities

- Describe document structuring, bank-statement and cash-flow analysis, business verification, fraud and risk signals, lender-specific policy evaluation, reviewer notes and overrides, audit history, and evidence-linked underwriting reports.
- Emphasize faster review, consistency, explainability, source-linked evidence, lender control, and human judgment.
- Use one persistent illustrative deal across product storytelling; label it clearly as illustrative.

## Audiences

- Write first for MCA funders, alternative lenders, revenue-based finance companies, and underwriting, credit, risk, and operations teams.
- Treat brokers, ISOs, commerce platforms, and embedded-finance partners as secondary audiences.
- Optimize the website for qualified walkthrough bookings rather than broad consumer signups.

## Proof and claim rules

- Do not fabricate customers, testimonials, pilots, contracts, revenue, certifications, integrations, performance metrics, approval rates, accuracy, guarantees, or regulatory endorsements.
- Do not publish the internally evaluated **200+ real-world underwriting files** proof point unless the user explicitly approves publication.
- Do not show public pricing.
- Keep claims concise, credible, buyer-focused, and evidence-based.

## Cevrynt × SHOPLINE

- Describe only a documented development and referral partnership around e-commerce merchant-underwriting workflows.
- Never imply a generally available live integration, investment, exclusivity, endorsement, automatic data sharing, universal merchant eligibility, or guaranteed funding.
